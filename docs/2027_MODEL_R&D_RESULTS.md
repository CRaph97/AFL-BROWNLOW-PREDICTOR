# 2027 Brownlow model — R&D results

Generated by `python -m src.validation.write_results` from `data/experiments/`. Plan and rules: `docs/2027_MODEL_R&D_PLAN.md`.

## 0. Findings (curated from the tables below)

All numbers are out-of-sample (train < test season), scored on the 2,689 matches and the player universe common to every model, test seasons 2012-2026 unless stated.

1. **Every 2027 candidate beats the Phase 4 / Production configuration on probability quality.** Paired bootstraps vs the baseline: Structural A improves 3-vote accuracy by +1.4pp (95% CI +0.2 to +2.6) and P3 log loss by 0.021 (CI 0.009 to 0.033); Performance ML B improves log loss by 0.043 (CI 0.026 to 0.059) and exact 3-2-1; accuracy gains for B are not significant. Brier improves from 0.0142 to 0.0124-0.0126 for every candidate.
2. **Champion by the pre-declared rules: Performance ML (B, XGBoost LambdaMART + inner-holdout temperature).** Pooled P3 log loss 1.239 vs 1.283, wins 13 of 15 seasons including every recent season, season MAE 0.874 vs 0.894, Spearman 0.785 vs 0.771. Structural A is the most accurate single model on the 3-vote pick (59.7% vs 58.9%) and is promotable; the two are kept separate because they win different metrics.
3. **The ensemble is justified.** Weights learned only from earlier seasons' OOF predictions are stable (B ~0.60-0.65, A ~0.20-0.40, C ~0.00-0.17 shrinking to zero) and the learned pool beats B on log loss in 12 of 12 seasons, A in 10 of 12 and C in 11 of 12 (pooled 1.226 vs 1.243 for B), with accuracy 59.9%. It beats A on accuracy in only 7 of 12 seasons, so A stays the headline pick model and the ensemble the headline probability model.
4. **Stats-only learned model (C) is not promoted** but is a strong benchmark: 59.2% accuracy from same-match features alone equals A on the pick and beats the baseline; its log loss is no better than the baseline and its season MAE is worst, and the ensemble gives it near-zero weight. Nonlinearity on the same strict features (C-ML) buys log loss (1.262) but not accuracy.
5. **Most valuable features (recent-8 ablation, 2017-2026).** Match context (win, margin) is the only family whose removal costs more than 1pp of accuracy (-1.8pp, +0.067 log loss, worse in 6/10 seasons). Point-in-time reputation (prior-season votes per game) -0.6pp, extended match-relative transforms +0.012 log loss, team strength -0.4pp, dominance / vote-competition +0.006 log loss are small but mostly consistent. XGBoost's importance is dominated by the fixed composite `impact_z` (40%), its match rank (16%) and disposals z-score.
6. **Features that failed ablation.** Role dummies and role interactions: removing them slightly *improves* accuracy (+0.3pp / +0.1pp) and log loss once dominance and relative features exist. Era index: zero effect. The legacy same-season reputation columns (not deployable) add nothing beyond point-in-time reputation, so nothing is lost by removing them. Footywire advanced stats (2015+) add -0.45pp accuracy but no log loss gain.
7. **2026 lessons tested historically.** (a) Ruck over-prediction is real but recent: the Structural model's ruck EV bias was ~0 or negative every season 2012-2021, then +0.5, +1.3, +2.5, +1.0 votes in 2022-2025 and +0.2 in 2026; the stats-only model over-credits rucks most (+1.5 in the recent window). (b) The key-defender blind spot persists in every model (A finds the 3-voter 29% of the time vs 63% for midfielders; only 1-9 key-defender 3-voters per season); 2026's 86% on medium defenders is an outlier year against a 15-67% history, so no defender correction is justified. (c) Losing-team 3-voters are the largest systematic miss: 47% for A vs 61% otherwise, and the ML models are worse (38-40%), i.e. they lean harder on the result.
8. **Calibration.** Raw P3 is already well calibrated (ECE 0.003-0.005) but walk-forward Platt scaling of the utilities cuts log loss for every model (A 1.271 to 1.234, B 1.250 to 1.230) with accuracy unchanged; isotonic helps A but hurts B. Platt on the ensemble is the candidate deployment configuration, to be confirmed on 2027.
9. **Disagreement is informative.** When the three candidates name the same 3-voter (76% of matches) the consensus is right 67% of the time; when two disagree with one, 38%; when all three differ (1.4%), 38% but some model is right 86% of the time. Season-level |EV gap| between A and B tracks error: MAE 0.6 at gaps under 1 vote, 2.8 at 1-3, 4.6-4.9 at 3-6. Disagreement is a valid uncertainty flag; no probability was modified.
10. **Data gaps.** No umpire appointment data exists in any held source; advanced stats begin 2015; role labels are lagged proxies (unknown for 17% of rows); Jack Ross's 2026 votes rely on a name-and-team fallback.


## 1. Baseline reproduction

The new metric code, run on the frozen 2026 Production and Objective match probabilities, gives 3-vote accuracy 0.7035 (frozen evaluation 0.7005) and 0.5024 (0.5024); reproduced = True. Production's denominator differs by 8 matches because its roster omits some 2/1-vote getters (first-game exclusion), which the new per-match table skips rather than scores.

Phase 4 configuration (legacy 68 CORE features, recent-8 window) re-run walk-forward 2015-2025: mean 3-vote accuracy 0.5750, exact 3-2-1 0.0913 (docs/MODEL_BACKTEST.md reported 0.5750 / 0.0913 for the same window; differences come from median vs dropna handling being identical here, but the 2026-extended table's re-normalised round labels and the analytic-gradient optimiser converging further).
